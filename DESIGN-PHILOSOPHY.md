# Design philosophy

Clarity for today. Continuity for tomorrow.

- Reduce cognitive load; shallow navigation and progressive disclosure.
- Capture first, organize later. Only a task title is required.
- Distinguish deadlines from intentions; no guilt or streak pressure.
- AI proposes; the user decides. No silent changes.
- Modern eclectic boho with midcentury influences: peacock blue, spring grass, warm ivory, walnut and raspberry plum. Clean sans serif functional text; restrained expressive accents. Space before borders. Accessible contrast and keyboard navigation.
- Private data stays outside public source code. No secrets in frontend code.

## Command Central

Command Central is a daily staging area, not an exhaustive dashboard. It supports orienting, setting intentions, assembling tasks and a temporary toolkit of relevant links, documents, notes and resources, then engaging with the day.

## Continuity as a core design goal

Help users maintain continuity between intentions and action while preserving curiosity, spontaneity and autonomy. With explicit permission, future assistance may learn routines, recurring commitments, planning patterns and preferences from actual behavior. Offer optional checkpoints, re-entry support and suggestions; distinguish a deliberate change of priorities from an unintentional diversion without assuming either. Never diagnose, shame, enforce routines or silently rearrange plans. Learned patterns should be inspectable, correctable, optional and deletable. Pattern learning is a future capability, not implemented in the prototype.

## Adaptive challenge

Personalization must not merely reinforce existing tendencies. With permission, the Scout should notice recurring patterns and offer proportionate, evidence-based questions about whether they support the user's stated intentions. Treat interpretations as hypotheses, not diagnoses or facts. Question the suitability of routines and the system itself as well as behavior. Invite the user to confirm, correct, dismiss or revise a pattern; allow adjustment of challenge frequency and intensity. Never infer readiness solely from inactivity or treat deliberate deferral as failure. No automatic changes to priorities, pins or routines. Support ongoing reflection and adaptation, not compliance.

## Intentional visibility

Daily priorities start fresh, with optional explained suggestions. Pins are a separate persistent awareness mechanism: keeping something visible does not create an obligation or deadline. The daily toolkit gathers resources for today's intentions. These are independent dimensions, not mandatory workflow stages.

## Scout

Scout is the AI companion within The Clearing. Notice. Connect. Preserve. Guide. Scout supports orientation, continuity, exploration and intentional action without taking ownership of the user's decisions.

## Vaktari, the Scout of The Clearing

Vaktari is the named AI companion; Scout remains his role. His visual identity is an original peacock-blue dragon with bronze accents, intelligent and approachable. The dragon–rider metaphor emphasizes partnership, complementary perspective, constructive challenge and human agency. See farther. Remember what matters. Challenge when necessary.


## Vaktari's observations and memory
With the user's permission, Vaktari occasionally initiates thoughtful observations about patterns in work, thinking, decisions, and personal growth. Distinguish evidence from interpretation; invite correction, dismissal, or reflection. Never treat inferred traits as established facts. Keep observations inspectable, editable, and removable; respect scope and frequency preferences. No diagnostic claims or guilt. Separate continuity memory, personal understanding, shared conversational history, and fictional character memory. Keep private data out of the public repository; authenticated storage and explicit memory controls are implementation prerequisites.

## Stable architecture, flexible activity

The Clearing is a workspace, not a website. Use a quiet, ebook-like editorial layout with a persistent left navigation anchor, consistent line icons, active-page highlighting and a house-only Home link to Command Central in the upper right. Shared shell files centralize navigation and visual styling. Preserve readable content before editing controls, generous writing space, and progressive disclosure. Prefer whitespace and restrained accents over decorative cards. Command Central is dynamic; The Clearing is stable; Vaktari bridges the two. Suggestions may adapt to the user without silently rearranging the environment. Sidebar collapse and persistent Keep in View shortcuts are future refinements.
